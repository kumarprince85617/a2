Atelier Specialties

Curated Culinary Offerings

Five distinct avenues of bespoke stone fruit collaboration.

Patisserie Glazing Reductions

Ultra-smooth, seedless apricot purees reduced to exactly 68-Brix for pastry chefs demanding mirror-like tarts and petit fours.

Orchard Kernel Pralines

Caramelized organic apricot kernels infused with sea salt, providing rich nutty crunch for luxury dessert courses.

Vintage Barrel Allocations

Exclusive single-harvest reserves reserved annually for private dining clubs and international culinary institutions.

Botanical Nectar Clarification

Cold-pressed nectar extractions formulated for zero-proof mixology and luxury banquet welcome aperitifs.

Commemorative Jar Packaging

Hand-poured beeswax seals, Italian calligraphy labels, and embossed wooden presentation cases for milestone celebrations.
